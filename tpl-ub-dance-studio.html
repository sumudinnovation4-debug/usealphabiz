  1. {{t39|1}}

    {{t40|Walk in on your own}}

    {{t41|Most people do. Say hi at the desk, leave your coat in a locker and keep your trainers on.}}

  2. {{t42|2}}

    {{t43|Warm up in the circle}}

    {{t44|Ten easy minutes to the night's playlist. Everybody is busy with their own feet, so nobody is looking at yours.}}

    {{t46|Learn eight counts}}

    {{t47|The teacher breaks one short routine into eight beats and repeats it until it sticks, then puts the music back on.}}

{{t144|I have never danced. Is that a problem?}}

{{t145|That is exactly who the beginners' classes are for. About half of every class started with us in the last six months.}}

{{t146|Do I need a partner?}}

{{t147|No. In salsa, bachata and swing partners rotate every song, and the other styles are danced solo in a group.}}

{{t148|What should I wear?}}

{{t149|Clothes you can move in and clean trainers or socks. For heels, bring a pair with a strap or borrow one of ours.}}

{{t150|How do I book?}}

{{t151|Use the form below for your free class. After that, book any class ahead or pay at the desk if there is space.}}

{{t152|Is there an age limit?}}

{{t153|Classes are for adults over 18. Our oldest regular started swing at seventy-four.}}

{{t154|Is the studio accessible?}}

{{t155|Both rooms are on the ground floor with step-free access, and there is an accessible toilet next to the changing rooms.}}
