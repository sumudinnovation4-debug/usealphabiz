{{t122|What happens in a blind session?}}

{{t123|Fifty minutes at the table with a host and a dozen strips. You keep the ones you return to; we reveal names, makers and prices at the end. A session costs ₦12,500 taken off any bottle you buy.}}

{{t124|Do I have to buy anything?}}

{{t125|No. Many people leave with a card of numbers and nothing else. The strips you choose are yours to take home.}}

{{t126|Can I smell by post?}}

{{t127|Yes. The Blind Set sends six sealed vials with numbered cards, so you can do the same ritual at your own table.}}

{{t128|Where do you ship?}}

{{t129|Within the Nigeria and the EU, in recyclable card. Extraits travel as liquids, so we pack them by hand on Mondays.}}

{{t130|Can I return a bottle?}}

{{t131|Unopened bottles within 30 days. Opened ones we cannot sell again, but we will swap them for a set of samples.}}
