{{t144|Do films start on time?}}

{{t145|Yes. We show one short film chosen to go with the feature, then the film. No adverts. If you are late, a member of staff will seat you quietly.}}

{{t146|Can I take drinks in?}}

{{t147|Anything from our bar, in our glasses. Please keep hot food out of Screen Two: the sofas are old.}}

{{t148|Are both screens accessible?}}

{{t149|Both have step-free access, wheelchair spaces and a hearing loop. Every Wednesday evening screening is captioned, and relaxed screenings run on Wednesday afternoons.}}

{{t150|Can I swap or refund a ticket?}}

{{t151|Up to two hours before the film, for anything else or your money back. Members can swap until the doors open.}}

{{t152|Why do some films look scratched?}}

{{t153|Because they are on film. A 35mm print shows its age a little; we find the best copy we can and handle it with gloves.}}

{{t154|Can we hire a screen?}}

{{t155|Screen Two seats 48 and can be hired on weekday mornings and Mondays, with your film or one of ours.}}
