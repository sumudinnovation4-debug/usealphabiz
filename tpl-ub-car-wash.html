{{t18|How a wash goes}}

{{t19|Same order every time, whatever the car. It is slower than a machine, and that is the point.}}

  1. {{t20|Pre-rinse and snow foam}}

    {{t21|We rinse loose grit off from the roof down, then cover the car in thick snow foam and let it dwell for five minutes to lift the dirt before anything touches the paint.}}

    {{t22|10 minutes}}

  2. {{t23|Wheels and arches first}}

    {{t24|Wheels get their own bucket, brushes and mitts, so brake dust never meets your paintwork. Arches are flushed out and tyres scrubbed.}}

    {{t25|8 minutes}}

  3. {{t26|Two-bucket contact wash}}

    {{t27|A lambswool mitt, one panel at a time, roof to sills. After every panel the mitt is rinsed in clean water before it goes back into the shampoo.}}

    {{t28|12 minutes}}

  4. {{t29|Rinse, top down}}

    {{t30|A low-pressure flood rinse sheets the water off glass and panels and carries the last of the suds out of shuts, badges and mirrors.}}

    {{t31|4 minutes}}

  5. {{t32|Dried by hand}}

    {{t33|Twisted-loop towels for the panels and warm air for grilles, badges and mirrors, so nothing drips or spots on the way home.}}

    {{t34|6 minutes}}

{{t144|Before you come}}

{{t145|Rather ask a person? Call the bay on}} {{t146|⟦phone⟧}}.

{{t147|Do I need to book?}}

{{t148|Washes take walk-ins until 4pm when a bay is free, but booked cars go first. Valets and protection are by booking only.}}

{{t149|Can I wait while you wash?}}

{{t150|Yes. There's a warm room with coffee and Wi-Fi. For anything longer than a mini valet we'll text you when the car is ready.}}

{{t151|Do you pressure-wash the paint?}}

{{t152|Only for the pre-rinse, at low pressure and a safe distance. Anything that touches the paint is a mitt or a towel.}}

{{t153|Can you do vans and campers?}}

{{t154|Anything up to 2.9 metres tall fits the covered bay. Taller than that, call us and we'll wash it in the yard.}}

{{t155|What if it rains on the way home?}}

{{t156|Rain beads off a clean, sealed car. If something we missed shows up in daylight, bring it back within 48 hours and we'll redo it free.}}
