{{t166|Can I try a pen before I buy it?}}

{{t167|Yes. Every pen we sell has a tester at the counter, with a pad of the papers we stock. Bring your own notebook if you want to see how a nib behaves on it.}}

{{t168|Do you post outside the Nigeria?}}

{{t169|We post across the Nigeria in two to three working days and to most of Europe in about a week. Bottled ink cannot fly, so it travels by road only.}}

{{t170|Can you repair a fountain pen?}}

{{t171|We clean, flush and adjust nibs in the shop, usually while you wait. Older pens that need new sacs or parts go to a restorer we trust in Jos.}}

{{t172|Can I pause the Monthly ⟦name⟧?}}

{{t173|Pause, skip or stop it by email before the last Monday of the month. Nothing is charged for a month you skip.}}

{{t174|How small can a print run be?}}

{{t175|Fifty cards or sheets is our smallest run. Most wedding orders fall between eighty and two hundred.}}

{{t176|Are the evenings for complete beginners?}}

{{t177|Yes. Every evening starts from the first stroke, and every material is included in the price.}}
