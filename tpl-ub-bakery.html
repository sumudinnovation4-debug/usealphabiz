{{t126|Fair questions}}

{{t127|Asked at the counter at least once a day.}}

{{t128|When do I need to pre-order a box?}}

{{t129|By 8 pm the night before. Orders after that go into the next morning’s bake, and we’ll tell you which day.}}

{{t130|Can I mix the six?}}

{{t131|Any six from this month’s list, up to two of the same bake. Breads count as one item each.}}

{{t132|Do you deliver?}}

{{t133|Within three miles of the shop, between 7 and 10 am, free on boxes, cakes and platters.}}

{{t134|What about allergies?}}

{{t135|Every month has a vegan bake and a gluten-free one. We bake with nuts and wheat every day, so we can’t promise a free-from kitchen.}}
