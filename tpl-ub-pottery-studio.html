{{t33|Mud to table in six steps}}

{{t34|Every ⟦name⟧ piece goes through the same six steps and two firings. It takes about three weeks, and none of it can be hurried.}}

  1. {{t35|Step 1}}

    {{t36|Wedge}}

    {{t37|Each block is kneaded on a canvas bench to push out trapped air, so nothing cracks or bursts in the kiln.}}

  2. {{t38|Step 2}}

    {{t39|Throw}}

    {{t40|The clay is centred on the wheel, opened and pulled up into a cylinder, then shaped into a bowl, cup or plate.}}

  3. {{t41|Step 3}}

    {{t42|Trim}}

    {{t43|Leather-hard pieces go back on the wheel upside down to cut a clean foot ring and lose extra weight.}}

  4. {{t44|Step 4}}

    {{t45|Bisque}}

    {{t46|A first, slow firing to 1000°C turns dry clay into porous bisque that can soak up glaze.}}

  5. {{t47|Step 5}}

    {{t48|Glaze}}

    {{t49|Pieces are dipped by hand in one of our house glazes; each foot is waxed so it never sticks to the kiln shelf.}}

  6. {{t50|Step 6}}

    {{t51|Fire}}

    {{t52|The glaze firing runs overnight to 1240°C. We open the kiln the next afternoon, never sooner.}}

{{/sec_process}} {{#sec_glazes}}

{{t53|Four glazes, mixed in the back room}}

{{t54|We weigh and sieve every bucket ourselves. Each glaze is food safe, tested in the dishwasher and fired on our own clay body.}}

  • {{t55|Cobalt}}

    {{t56|A deep, glossy blue that breaks pale where it thins over the rims. The glaze most of our restaurant plates wear.}}

{{t130|Questions}}

+
{{t131|Do I need any experience for a class?}}+

{{t132|None at all. The taster evening and the six-week course both start from the very beginning, and the groups are small enough that everyone gets help on the wheel.}}

{{t133|What should I wear?}}+

{{t134|Clothes you do not mind getting clay on, and short nails if you can. We lend aprons and towels; clay washes out of cotton easily.}}

{{t135|When can I collect what I made?}}+

{{t136|Three to four weeks after your class, once the pieces have been trimmed, bisque fired, glazed and fired again. We email you when they are on the collection shelf.}}

{{t137|Is your tableware dishwasher safe?}}+

{{t138|Yes. All four house glazes are food safe and tested in commercial dishwashers and microwaves. Unglazed outsides are best washed by hand.}}

{{t139|How big does a commission have to be?}}+

{{t140|We start at about 40 pieces of one shape. Samples take four weeks and a full service ten to twelve, and we keep your glaze recipe on file for reorders.}}

{{t141|Do you sell gift vouchers?}}+

{{t142|For any class or any amount. Email us and we will send a printable voucher the same day; it is valid for a year.}}
