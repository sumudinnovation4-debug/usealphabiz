{{/sec_returns}} {{#sec_promises}}

{{t80|The asterisk is the promise, not the small print.}}

  1. {{t81|Nothing flown in.}}

    {{t82|If it can't come by road from within forty miles, it isn't in the box. That's why there are no bananas.}}

  2. {{t83|Nothing heated under glass.}}

    {{t84|Tunnels keep the rain off; they never burn gas. Tomatoes arrive in July, not in March.}}

  3. {{t85|No plastic, not even the tape.}}

    {{t86|Paper bags, a waxed cotton wrap for the leaves, and a crate that comes back.}}

  4. {{t87|Growers set the price.}}

    {{t88|Each farm names its price before the season starts, and the box price is built up from there.}}

  5. {{t89|Picked the day it travels.}}

    {{t90|Leaves and roots leave the field the morning they reach you. Only squash and onions are cured, on the farm.}}

  6. {{t91|Skip or stop any week.}}

    {{t92|No contract and no minimum. Pause for a holiday from your account up to Sunday midnight.}}

{{t108|When does my box arrive?}}

{{t109|Each area has one delivery day a week, Tuesday to Friday. Check your postcode below; vans usually reach you between three and seven in the evening.}}

{{t110|What if I'm out?}}

{{t111|Tell us a safe place when you sign up: a porch, a neighbour, behind the bins. Boxes are packed to sit outside for an evening, even in summer.}}

{{t112|Can I choose what goes in?}}

{{t113|The box follows what is ready, but you can swap up to three things a week and keep a list of what you never want sent.}}

{{t114|How do returns work?}}

{{t115|Leave last week's crate on the step; the driver folds it and takes it back. If one goes missing nothing happens, we would just like it back.}}

{{t116|Is everything certified organic?}}

{{t117|Yes. Every farm we buy from is certified, and each grower's licence number is printed on the recipe card with their name.}}

{{t118|How do I pause or cancel?}}

{{t119|From your account, any time up to Sunday midnight for the next week. No calls and no forms.}}
