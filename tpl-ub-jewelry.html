{{t115|How long does a commission take?}}

{{t116|Six to eight weeks from the first sketch for most rings; wedding bands about four. If there's a date, tell us at the first sitting and we'll plan backwards from it.}}

{{t117|What if my gold isn't enough for the piece?}}

{{t118|We weigh it at the valuation and top it up with recycled gold. You pay only for the difference, priced per gram on the day.}}

{{t119|Will my piece be hallmarked?}}

{{t120|Yes. Everything over the legal weight is sent to the Enugu Assay Office and comes back with its hallmark before you collect it.}}

{{t121|Do you buy gold too?}}

{{t122|We do, at the day's rate, and we'll always give you the figure in writing. Most people decide to remake instead once they've seen the sketches.}}

{{t123|Can I see the work in progress?}}

{{t124|Every commission includes a visit to approve the wax model and another before the stone is set. Many clients drop in more often; the kettle's always on.}}
