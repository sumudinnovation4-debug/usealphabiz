{{t144|Do you deliver, and how fast?}}

{{t145|Across the Nigeria in three to five working days, packed in paper and card. Kaduna orders over ₦75,000 come in our own van, often the next day.}}

{{t146|Can you fit the lamps as well?}}

{{t147|Yes. Our electrician fits pendants and wall lights across Kaduna from ₦32,500 a visit. Plug-in lamps need nobody.}}

{{t148|What does a lighting plan cost?}}

{{t149|Nothing with an order over ₦300,000. Otherwise it is ₦45,000 and the ₦45,000 comes off any lamps you buy from the plan within a year.}}

{{t150|Which bulb should I buy?}}

{{t151|For living rooms and bedrooms, warm white and dimmable. Bring the old bulb in or send a photo of the fitting, and we will match the base and the light.}}

{{t152|Can I return a lamp?}}

{{t153|Within thirty days, unused and in its box. Made-to-order shades and repaired lamps cannot come back, and we say so before you pay.}}
