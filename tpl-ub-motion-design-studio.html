{{t49|Every brand gets a tempo}}

{{t50|Before we animate anything, we choose how your brand moves: how fast it arrives, how hard it stops, how long it holds. Three of the tempos we have written so far:}}

  • {{t51|Glide}}

    {{t52|A long ease-out. Nothing arrives in a hurry; for brands that should feel inevitable.}}

  • {{t53|Snap}}

    {{t54|Fast in, hard stop, a beat of stillness. For brands that make decisions.}}

  • {{t55|Pulse}}

    {{t56|An even swing with a heartbeat on top. For brands that live with their audience every day.}}

{{t123|What is a motion system?}}

{{t124|A short set of rules for how your brand moves: easing curves, timing, transitions, how type and logo enter and leave. It comes as a document, project files and sample animations your team or any agency can use.}}

{{t125|Do you work with brands outside the Netherlands?}}

{{t126|Most of our clients are elsewhere in Europe and in North America. Workshops run in person or remotely; reviews are always remote.}}

{{t127|Can you work with our existing agency?}}

{{t128|Yes. We often write the motion system and the launch film, then hand the rules to the agency that runs your campaigns.}}

{{t129|Who owns the files?}}

{{t130|You do. Masters, project files and the motion rules are yours from the final invoice on.}}

{{t131|How soon can you start?}}

{{t132|A sprint usually starts within three weeks of the first call, a season within six.}}
