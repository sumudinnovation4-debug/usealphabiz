{{t132|Before you ride.}}

{{t135|Which licence do I need?}}

{{t136|A full car licence lets you ride any ⟦name⟧. Without one, you need a CBT certificate from the age of sixteen; we can book the course for you.}}

{{t137|How far does it really go in winter?}}

{{t138|Cold batteries give up about a fifth of their range, so plan on sixty kilometres on a frosty week. Charging indoors keeps the cells warm and helps.}}

{{t139|Can I charge it in a flat?}}

{{t140|Yes. The battery lifts out and plugs into any ordinary socket with the charger from the box. A spare battery doubles your range if you have nowhere to park near a socket.}}

{{t141|Is it all right in the rain?}}

{{t142|The motor and battery are sealed, so a Kano downpour is no trouble. Riding through deep standing water is the one thing we ask you to avoid.}}

{{t143|What if it is stolen?}}

{{t144|Every ⟦name⟧ has a tracker and an alarm in the app, and it will not start without your phone or key card nearby. Monthly riders get a replacement within a day.}}
