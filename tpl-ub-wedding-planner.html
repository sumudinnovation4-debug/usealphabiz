{{t147|How far ahead should we book?}}

{{t148|For full planning, twelve to eighteen months. For the day itself, eight weeks is enough, though summer Saturdays are usually taken a year ahead.}}

{{t149|Do you only plan garden weddings?}}

{{t150|Mostly gardens, orchards and glasshouses within ninety minutes of Bath. We have also planned barns, a boathouse and one library. Ask us.}}

{{t151|What happens if it rains?}}

{{t152|Every wedding has a written rain plan by the third month: a covered place for the ceremony, umbrellas in the right colour, and a time on the day at which we decide.}}

{{t153|Can we bring our own suppliers?}}

{{t154|Of course. We meet them, share the run sheet and make sure they know where to park the van and when the speeches start.}}

{{t155|How many weddings do you take?}}
