{{t212|Do I need to book?}}

{{t213|For a table, yes; we hold it for fifteen minutes. The soju bar and the ageing counter are walk-in only, and there is usually a seat after ten.}}

{{t214|Will I go home smelling of smoke?}}

{{t215|Less than you think. Every grill has its own hood that pulls the smoke straight up, and we hand out a coat bag at the door. Your hair is another matter.}}

{{t216|Someone in our group does not eat meat}}

{{t217|Ask for the garden set: king oyster mushrooms, tofu, rice cakes and every banchan, cooked on a grill that never sees meat.}}

{{t218|What about allergens?}}

{{t219|Soy, sesame, wheat and fish sauce run through most marinades. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will mark every plate that comes to your table.}}

{{t220|Can we bring children?}}

{{t221|Until eight, yes. We bring a high chair and keep the grill nearest small hands for the cooks to work.}}

{{t222|How does a group of twelve work?}}

{{t223|The back room seats twenty. Book the long table set and we take a ₦5,000 deposit a head, taken off the bill on the night.}}
