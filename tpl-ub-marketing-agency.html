    {{t30|The unpolished premium}}

  4. {{t32|Cooling · 7 of 9 markets}}

    {{t33|Borrowed authority}}

    {{t34|Generalists who endorse everything are losing pull. People now ask who has spent ten years on one narrow thing, and they listen to that person instead.}}

  5. {{t35|Rising fast · 9 of 9 markets}}

    {{t36|Questions go out loud}}

    {{t37|What used to be typed into a search bar is now asked in comment threads, voice notes and assistants. Brands need answers that people can quote back.}}

    {{t40|Impressions are up and memory is down. Media bought without a reason to remember is, in one respondent’s words, paying for weather.}}
