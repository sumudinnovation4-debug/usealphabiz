{{t111|Do you charge for the first visit?}}

{{t112|No. The visit and the written quote are free, and there is no obligation to book the work.}}

{{t113|Are you registered and insured?}}

{{t114|Yes. Work that has to be notified to building control is notified by us, every job comes with its certificate, and we carry five million pounds of public liability cover.}}

{{t115|Can we stay in the house during a rewire?}}

{{t116|Most families do. We work room by room, keep power on in the rooms you use each night and plan the kitchen and bathroom days with you.}}

{{t117|Will you make good after cutting into walls?}}

{{t118|Yes. Chases and holes are filled and sanded flush, ready for paint. We do not decorate, but we can recommend people who do.}}

{{t119|Which areas do you cover?}}

{{t120|Ibadan and the villages around it, out to Hathersage, Stocksbridge and Dronfield. Ask if you are a little further out.}}

{{t122|Often. We do the first fix before the plasterer, come back for the second fix and talk to your other trades directly, so you do not have to carry messages.}}
