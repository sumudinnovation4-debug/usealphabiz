{{t137|Before you come aboard}}

{{t138|Ask the kiosk}}

{{t139|Whoever is on the kiosk at Pier 3 picks up from half past eight until the last boat is in.}}

{{t140|Call ⟦phone⟧}}
{{t141|What should I bring?}}

{{t142|Something warm, even in August. We have blankets, but a hat and a windproof layer make the run to the light far nicer.}}

{{t143|Do you sail in bad weather?}}

{{t144|In light rain, yes: Kittiwake is covered. If the harbour master closes the bar or the swell is too big, we text you by eight in the morning and refund in full.}}

{{t145|Can I bring my dog?}}

{{t146|Dogs on leads are welcome on Marigold and Kittiwake. Hesper is too small for them, sorry.}}

{{t147|Is it all right for small children?}}

{{t148|Yes. There are lifejackets in every size on board, and under-fours sail free on a lap.}}

{{t149|Where exactly do we meet?}}

{{t150|At the kiosk on Pier 3, North Quay, ten minutes' walk from the station. Please be there a quarter of an hour before you sail.}}

{{t151|Can I change my booking?}}

{{t152|Up to a day before departure, free of charge, online or at the kiosk.}}
