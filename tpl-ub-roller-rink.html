{{t43|Sixteen hundred boards of hard maple}}

{{t44|The floor is the rink. Ours is northern hard maple, tongue and groove, laid over a sprung timber frame so your knees forgive the falls.}}

  • {{t45|Sprung, not slab}}

    {{t46|Timber joists under every board give a softer landing than concrete.}}

  • {{t47|Sealed every August}}

    {{t48|We close from 4 to 18 August, sand it back and lay seven coats.}}

  • {{t49|Skates only past the rail}}

    {{t50|No street shoes on the maple, ever. Socks are fine for birthday photos.}}

{{t147|Before your first lap}}

{{t148|Still wondering? Call the counter during any session and someone will pick up between songs.}}

{{t149|Do I need my own skates?}}

{{t150|No. Rental quads are included in every ticket. Your own quads or inline skates are welcome if the wheels are clean and the toe stops are not metal.}}

{{t151|Is there a minimum age?}}

{{t152|Children of any age can skate with an adult on the floor. Lessons start at six, and Tuesday nights are eighteen and over.}}

{{t153|Do I have to wear a helmet?}}

{{t154|Helmets are required for under-twelves and lent free at the counter. Wrist guards are a good idea for anyone on their first few sessions.}}

{{t155|Can I just watch?}}

{{t156|Yes, the benches along the rail are free for anyone with a skater in the family. The snack bar is open to everyone.}}

{{t157|Can I pay at the door?}}

{{t158|Always. Cash or card, no booking needed for public sessions. Courses and parties are booked ahead.}}
