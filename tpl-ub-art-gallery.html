{{t157|Anything else: ask at the door, or write to us before the evening.}}

{{t158|Is it really open until two?}}

{{t159|Yes, Thursday to Sunday. Last entry is at 01:30; the rooms close at 02:00 and the garden bar ten minutes earlier.}}

{{t160|Do I need a ticket for the Friday screening?}}

{{t161|Seats are free with the Night Pass and €9 at the door. The hall holds ninety people; pass holders can reserve until Thursday noon.}}

{{t162|Is the building accessible?}}

{{t163|All four rooms and the hall are step-free, with a lift to the first floor. The light stays low, so ask at the door for a hand torch.}}

{{t164|Can I bring children?}}

{{t165|Before 22:00, with an adult. After that the rooms are for visitors aged sixteen and over.}}

{{t166|Can I take photographs?}}

{{t167|Yes, without flash. The Red Room asks for no photographs while its installation is running.}}
