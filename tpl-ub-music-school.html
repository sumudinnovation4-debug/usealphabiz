{{t156|Ask a mentor}}

{{t157|What do I need for an online lesson?}}

{{t158|Your instrument, a laptop or tablet with a camera, and headphones. We send a five-minute setup guide that makes the sound clear enough for both of you to play together.}}

{{t159|How young can a student start?}}

{{t160|Piano from six, guitar and drums from eight, voice from ten. Younger children are welcome in a trial lesson with a parent beside them.}}

{{t161|Can I switch mentors?}}

{{t162|Yes, at any time and without a reason. Your notes and recordings move to the new mentor so nothing starts again from zero.}}

{{t163|How does the free lesson work?}}

{{t164|You pick a time, we match you with a mentor, and you spend thirty minutes playing and talking about goals. No card is needed to book it.}}

{{t165|Can I pause my plan?}}

{{t166|Pause for up to two months a year from your account. Lessons you have already paid for wait for you.}}
