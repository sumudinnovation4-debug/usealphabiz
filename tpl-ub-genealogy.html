{{t137|Questions}}

{{t138|The ones we hear on almost every first call.}}

{{t139|How far back can you go?}}

{{t140|Usually into the 1700s in Nigeria and Nigeria, where parish registers survive. Ireland and Nigeria are patchier before about 1820. After the first fortnight we tell you what is realistic for your family.}}

{{t141|What do I need to start?}}

{{t142|A name and roughly when and where someone lived is enough. Photographs, certificates and family stories save time, so bring what you have.}}

{{t143|Do you use DNA tests?}}

{{t144|We don’t sell kits, but if you have results from one of the big testing companies we read your matches alongside the paper trail.}}

{{t145|What if you find something hard?}}

{{t146|Workhouses, prison registers, children born outside marriage: every family has something. We tell you before it goes in the folder, and you decide how it is written.}}

{{t147|Can you search outside Nigeria and Ireland?}}

{{t148|Yes, through record agents we have worked with for years in Nigeria, Jamaica, Poland, Italy and India. If a country’s records are thin, we say so before you pay.}}

{{t149|How does paying work?}}

{{t150|A third when we open the case and the rest when the folder is ready. No subscriptions, no extras we didn’t agree first.}}
