{{t35|One sheet, two passes.}}

{{t36|From your file to a dry stack in five stations. Two hundred A3 posters take about forty minutes of drum time.}}

  1. {{t37|1}}

    {{t38|Artwork}}

    {{t39|One page per colour, everything in black. Mark which page is blue and which is pink; we set the screens.}}

    {{t40|You · whenever it is ready}}

  2. {{t41|2}}

    {{t42|Masters}}

    {{t43|The duplicator burns each page into a stencil: a thin film with thousands of pinholes, one master per ink.}}

    {{t44|Machine · 30 seconds each}}

  3. {{t45|3}}

    {{t46|Blue pass}}

    {{t47|The blue drum turns at up to 130 sheets a minute, pressing ink through its master onto every sheet.}}

    {{t48|First pass · ten minutes to dry}}

  4. {{t49|4}}

    {{t50|Pink pass}}

    {{t51|The same stack goes through again under the pink drum. We nudge the registration by hand until the marks meet.}}

    {{t52|Second pass · the colours appear}}

  5. {{t53|5}}

    {{t54|Dry, trim, fold}}

    {{t55|A night on the rack, then the guillotine, the folder and the long-arm stapler. Packed flat in recycled card.}}

    {{t56|Ready the next working day}}

{{t150|Before you send a file.}}

{{t151|What file should I send?}}

{{t152|A PDF with one page per ink, everything in black. Make page one the blue and page two the pink; we turn greys into halftone on the machine.}}

{{t153|Can you match a brand colour?}}

{{t154|No, and that is the point: riso inks are fixed. We keep Medium Blue and Fluorescent Pink on the drums; tints and overprints give you sixteen more.}}

{{t155|What are the smallest and largest runs?}}

{{t156|Thirty copies is the smallest run worth a master; the largest we have printed is 3,000 flyers. Beyond that we will send you to an offset printer we trust.}}

{{t157|Why does every copy look a little different?}}

{{t158|The drum lays wet ink and the paper shifts by a hair between passes. A little misregistration is part of the look; if your design needs it tight, we run slower.}}

{{t159|Which papers work?}}

{{t160|Uncoated stock from 70 to 300 g, up to A3. Coated and glossy papers will not take the ink, so we keep a shelf of good uncoated ones for you to choose from.}}

{{t161|How long does a job take?}}

{{t162|Most runs are ready two working days after you approve the proof. Large runs and bound zines take up to five.}}
