{{t125|We paint for the light you have}}

{{t126|A colour is only half paint; the other half is the window. Before we choose anything, we watch your walls at four hours of the day.}}

  1. {{t127|Morning}}{{t128|Cool and clear. Blues look their bluest.}}
  2. {{t129|Noon}}{{t130|Flat and honest. The hour to judge a white.}}
  3. {{t131|Evening}}{{t132|Low and warm. Pinks blush and greys turn to sand.}}
  4. {{t133|Night}}{{t134|Lamps only. Now the ceiling sets the mood.}}

{{t149|Questions people ask}}

{{t150|Anything else, ask us on a colour walk or write a note below.}}

{{t151|Do you work outside Lyon?}}

{{t152|Yes. Colour walks are within two hours of Lyon by train. Drawings and light scores can be made anywhere from your photos, plans and a video call.}}

{{t153|Can we keep our furniture?}}

{{t154|Almost always. We draw around the pieces you love and tell you honestly which one is fighting the room.}}

{{t155|How long does a one-room drawing take?}}

{{t156|Three weeks from the first sitting to the specimen sheet, then usually two more until the painters have packed up.}}

{{t157|Do you sell the paints?}}

{{t158|Only within a commission, mixed to order in Lyon. Each project ends with a tin of every shade we used.}}

{{t159|What if we hate the colour?}}

{{t160|That is what the third sitting is for. We paint swatches a metre square on your own walls and change them until you stop frowning.}}
