{{t3|The round}}

{{t31|One round,}}{{t32|three temperatures}}

{{t33|Bathers here follow an old order. Repeat it three or four times; nobody keeps count but you.}}

  1. {{t34|Step 1}}

    {{t35|Heat}}

    {{t36|Twelve minutes on the upper bench.}}

    {{t37|Sit high and breathe slowly. Pour water on the stones when the room asks for it, and let the heat climb until your shoulders drop.}}

    {{t38|8–15 min · 80–95 °C}}

  2. {{t39|Step 2}}

    {{t40|Cold}}

    {{t41|Twenty steps to the lake.}}

    {{t42|Walk out along the jetty and go in to the shoulders. For one minute the cold shuts every thought out; that is the point of it.}}

    {{t43|1–2 min · 4–16 °C}}

  3. {{t44|Step 3}}

    {{t45|Rest}}

    {{t46|As long as it takes.}}

    {{t47|Wrap up by the stove, drink something warm and say nothing. The calm that arrives now is the reason you came.}}

    {{t48|15–20 min · by the window}}

{{t12|Questions}}

{{t152|Before}}{{t153|your first round}}

{{t154|Do I need to book?}}

{{t155|Weekends and evenings fill up, so book ahead. On weekday mornings you can usually walk in; ask at the desk.}}

{{t156|What should I bring?}}

{{t157|Nothing at all. A robe, towels, slippers and a locker are included. Bring a swimsuit if you prefer one for the lake.}}

{{t158|Is bathing mixed?}}

{{t159|Yes, every day except Tuesday evenings, which are for women only. Swimwear or a towel in all shared rooms.}}

{{t160|I am new to saunas. Where do I start?}}

{{t161|Begin in the herb steam and keep the first rounds short. A host walks first-timers through the round at ten and at three.}}

{{t162|Can I come if I am pregnant or have a heart condition?}}

{{t163|Please ask your doctor first. The steam room and the rest room are gentle; the smoke sauna and the lake are not.}}

{{t164|Do you sell gift cards?}}

{{t165|Yes, for any pass, by email or at the desk. They are valid for a year from the day you buy them.}}
