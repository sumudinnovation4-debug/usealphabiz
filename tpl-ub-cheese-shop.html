{{t55|What the cellar does}}

{{t56|Cheese leaves the farm young. The last weeks or months decide how it tastes, and they happen forty steps below the shop.}}

  1. {{t57|Step 1}}

    {{t58|Arrive}}

    {{t59|Wheels come in from the makers at a few weeks old and rest for a day before anyone touches them.}}

  2. {{t60|Step 2}}

    {{t61|Wash and brush}}

    {{t62|Washed rinds get a brine wipe twice a week; natural rinds are brushed so the moulds stay even.}}

  3. {{t63|Step 3}}

    {{t64|Turn}}

    {{t65|Every Thursday each wheel is turned by hand, so the moisture stays even through the paste.}}

  4. {{t66|Step 4}}

    {{t67|Taste with an iron}}

    {{t68|We pull a core with a cheese iron, taste it and put the plug back. Nothing goes upstairs before it’s right.}}

  5. {{t69|Step 5}}

    {{t70|Bring it up}}

    {{t71|Ready wheels go up to the counter, where they are cut to order and wrapped in waxed paper.}}

{{t148|Questions at the counter}}

{{t149|Anything else, ask us in the shop or by email.}}

  • {{t150|Do you post cheese?}}

    {{t151|Yes: Tuesday to Thursday, by next-day courier in insulated boxes, anywhere in mainland Nigeria. We don’t post on Fridays, so nothing sits in a depot over the weekend.}}

  • {{t152|How long will it keep?}}

    {{t153|Hard cheeses keep two to three weeks in their paper in the salad drawer; soft and fresh cheeses about a week. Re-wrap in fresh paper if the old one turns greasy.}}

  • {{t154|Can you cut a whole wheel for a wedding?}}

    {{t155|Gladly: stacked wheels, whole cheeses and long boards for any number of guests. Give us two weeks’ notice for the big wheels.}}

  • {{t156|Is it all raw-milk cheese?}}

    {{t157|Most of it, and every label says which. If you’re pregnant or avoiding raw milk, ask and we’ll point you to the pasteurised ones.}}

  • {{t158|Do you have vegetarian cheeses?}}

    {{t159|About half the counter uses vegetarian rennet, and the label strip says so.}}

  • {{t160|Can I bring my own containers?}}

    {{t161|Yes. We weigh your box first and cut straight into it.}}
