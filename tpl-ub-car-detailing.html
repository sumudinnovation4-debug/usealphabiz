[{{t100|Five days}}]

{{t101|A working week for one car.}}

{{t102|A full treatment runs Monday to Friday. At the end of each day you get a photo and the readings, so you know where the car is without having to ask.}}

  1. {{t103|Monday}}

    {{t104|Inspect and measure}}

    {{t105|Lamps, gauge and notes. Every chip, scratch and respray is photographed and agreed with you before we start.}}

  2. {{t106|Tuesday}}

    {{t107|Wash and decontaminate}}

    {{t108|Snow foam, a two-bucket wash, iron fallout and tar dissolved, then clay on every panel until the paint feels like glass.}}

  3. {{t109|Wednesday}}

    {{t110|Correct}}

    {{t111|Compound and polish, panel by panel, re-reading the gauge as we go. The day ends with a look under every lamp.}}

  4. {{t112|Thursday}}

    {{t113|Coat}}

    {{t114|A panel wipe, then two layers of ceramic, levelled by hand and cured under infrared overnight.}}

  5. {{t115|Friday}}

    {{t116|Finish and hand over}}

    {{t117|Glass, wheels and cabin, a last look under the lamps, and the keys handed back with the readings and an aftercare kit.}}

[{{t138|Questions}}]

{{t139|What owners ask before booking.}}

{{t140|How long will you have my car?}}

{{t141|Five working days for a full treatment, one to three for a single one. We agree the dates at the inspection and keep them.}}

{{t142|Will every scratch come out?}}

{{t143|We remove what the clear coat allows. A scratch through to the colour can be made much less visible, not erased — we show you which is which under the lamps before we start.}}

{{t144|How long does a ceramic coating last?}}

{{t145|Ours carries a five-year warranty with a free yearly check. How long it really lasts depends on washing, so we show you how at handover.}}

{{t146|Can I still use a car wash?}}

{{t147|Please avoid brushes. A touchless jet wash is fine in a pinch; a two-bucket hand wash, or our maintenance wash, is better.}}

{{t148|Do you collect the car?}}

{{t149|Yes, on a covered trailer, within thirty miles of Lagos. It is free with any five-day treatment.}}

{{t150|What does an inspection cost?}}

{{t151|Nothing. It takes about forty minutes, and you leave with your paint readings whether you book or not.}}
