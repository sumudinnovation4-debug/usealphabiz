{{t5|How hire works}}

{{t66|Four steps between our shelf and your pitch, and none of them asks for money up front.}}

  1. {{t67|01}}

    {{t68|Book}}

    {{t69|Choose kit online or ring the shop. We hold it for your dates, and nothing is charged until you collect.}}

  2. {{t70|02}}

    {{t71|Get fitted}}

    {{t72|Twenty minutes in the fitting room: boots on the slope, packs loaded to weight, bags matched to your height.}}

  3. {{t73|03}}

    {{t74|Go}}

    {{t75|Every hire leaves with a route card for the valleys around us and a phone number answered until nine at night.}}

  4. {{t76|04}}

    {{t77|Bring it back muddy}}

    {{t78|Drop it at the counter or the after-hours locker. Cleaning is on us; we only charge for damage beyond a stitch.}}

{{/sec_how}} {{#sec_fitting}}

{{t79|Fitted, not handed over.}}

{{t80|Kit that fits is kit you forget about on the hill. Every hire starts with twenty minutes in our fitting room, and it costs nothing.}}

  1. {{t81|Boots on a slope}}

    {{t82|We measure both feet, lace you in and walk you up and down our ramp until your heel stays put on the way down.}}

  2. {{t83|Packs loaded to weight}}

    {{t84|Your torso length sets the back system. We load the pack to what you will carry and settle the hip belt on your hips, not your waist.}}

  3. {{t85|Shells over your layers}}

    {{t86|Jackets are tried over the fleece you will wear, arms up, so the cuffs stay at your wrists and the hood turns with your head.}}

{{/sec_fitting}} {{#sec_care}}

{{t87|Between every hire, the same six checks.}}

{{t88|Nothing goes back on the shelf until it passes all six. Each piece carries a card with the date and the initials of whoever checked it.}}

  1. {{t89|Check 1 of 6}}

    {{t90|Washed}}

    {{t91|Tents rinsed, bags and shells washed with a technical cleaner, boots scrubbed and waxed.}}

  2. {{t92|Check 2 of 6}}

    {{t93|Dried for two days}}

    {{t94|Hung in the drying loft for forty-eight hours. Nothing is ever packed away damp.}}

  3. {{t95|Check 3 of 6}}

    {{t96|Seams tested}}

    {{t97|Every tent is pitched in the yard, sprayed with a hose and checked from the inside for drips.}}

  4. {{t98|Check 4 of 6}}

    {{t99|Zips run end to end}}

    {{t100|Sliders cleaned, pulls replaced and stiff teeth fixed before they can fail on a ridge.}}

  5. {{t101|Check 5 of 6}}

    {{t102|Stoves fired}}

    {{t103|Each stove is lit and its flame checked; seals and jets are replaced every season.}}

  6. {{t104|Check 6 of 6}}

    {{t105|Repaired in the workroom}}

    {{t106|Small tears are patched and re-taped the same week. Whatever we cannot trust, we retire.}}

{{t143|Questions people ask at the counter}}

{{t144|Do I need to book?}}

{{t145|For weekends from May to September, yes. Midweek we can usually kit you out on the day if you ring first.}}

{{t146|What if it rains the whole time?}}

{{t147|Bring it back wet. Drying and cleaning are part of every hire, and we would rather you didn’t try to dry a tent in a flat.}}

{{t148|Is there a deposit?}}

{{t149|We hold a card when you collect and release it when the kit is back. Nothing is charged before then.}}

{{t150|What happens if something breaks?}}

{{t151|Tell us. Normal wear is on us; we only charge for damage beyond a simple repair, and never more than the item is worth.}}

{{t152|Can you deliver to a campsite?}}

{{t153|Yes, to campsites and huts within twenty miles of the shop, for ₦7,500 each way.}}

{{t154|Can I keep the kit longer?}}

{{t155|Ring before your return day and we will extend the hire at the nightly rate, as long as nobody has booked it next.}}
