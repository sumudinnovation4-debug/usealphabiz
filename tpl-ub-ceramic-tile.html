{{t62|From wet clay to a loud floor.}}

  1. {{t63|Step 1}}

    {{t64|Press}}

    {{t65|White stoneware clay is pressed into a steel frame, one tile at a time, and trimmed square while it is still soft.}}

  2. {{t66|Step 2}}

    {{t67|Dry}}

    {{t68|Ten days on open racks. The clay loses its water slowly and evenly, so the tile stays flat and the edges stay sharp.}}

  3. {{t69|Step 3}}

    {{t70|Pour the frame}}

    {{t71|The frame glaze goes on through a mask. Raw, it looks chalky and pale; nobody can tell Signal from Raspberry yet.}}

  4. {{t72|Step 4}}

    {{t73|Set the core}}

    {{t74|The core glaze is laid in a second pass, with the ring left bare. Dot, square or full: the mask decides how much shows.}}

  5. {{t75|Step 5}}

    {{t76|Fire}}

    {{t77|Twelve hours up to 1,200 °C and a day to cool. The kiln turns chalk into colour, and the bare ring comes out white.}}

{{t128|Can ⟦name⟧ tiles go outside?}}

{{t129|Yes. Stoneware fired at 1,200 °C takes frost and sun. For outdoor floors and pool edges we fire the R11 non-slip finish, which mutes the glaze a little; ask for it with your samples.}}

{{t130|How much should I order?}}

{{t131|Your area plus 10% for cuts, 15% for a diagonal or a room with many corners. Every batch is fired once, so order the spares now: a later firing can differ by a shade.}}

{{t132|How long does an order take?}}

{{t133|Three to five weeks depending on the size, from the day you approve the proof tile. Delivery in Spain takes two days, across Europe four to six by pallet.}}

{{t134|Can you mix a glaze that is not on the list?}}

{{t135|For orders over 40 m² we match a colour you send us. It takes three test firings and adds two weeks; the glaze then joins your account for later orders.}}

{{t136|How do I clean them?}}

{{t137|Warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner. The glaze is sealed by the firing; the bare ring is vitrified too, so it needs no sealant and does not stain.}}

{{t138|Do you lay the tiles?}}

{{t139|No, but we send a laying plan with every order and work with tilers in Valencia, Barcelona and Madrid whom we can recommend.}}
