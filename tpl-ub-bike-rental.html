{{t7|Questions}}

{{t163|Anything else: ring the Station Arches stand, someone is in the workshop all day.}}

{{t164|Do I need to be fit to ride one?}}

{{t165|No. The motor helps as soon as you pedal, so you choose how much effort to put in. On the highest assist level most riders climb our steepest streets seated.}}

{{t166|How old do riders have to be?}}

{{t167|Sixteen or over to ride on your own. Children from one to seven can ride on the Hauler's seats with a parent pedalling.}}

{{t168|What if the battery runs low?}}

{{t169|The screen shows the range left. Ride to any stand and we swap the battery in a minute, free. A flat battery still leaves you a normal bike with light gears.}}

{{t170|Can I take a bike on the train?}}

{{t171|The Pocket folds and travels as luggage on every train. The other bikes go on Northern services outside the rush hours, space permitting.}}

{{t172|Do you take a deposit?}}

{{t173|We hold ₦100,000 on a card while the bike is out and release it when you return it. Nothing is taken unless there is damage beyond the ₦25,000 excess.}}
