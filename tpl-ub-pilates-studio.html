{{t13|Four ways onto the apparatus}}

{{t14|Most people start on the Reformer and add the others as their body asks for them. Every class is booked by the place, and every place comes with a teacher's eyes on you.}}

  1. {{t15|Reformer}}

    {{t16|A sliding carriage on springs, with straps for hands and feet. The springs resist both ways, so every movement is worked on the way out and again on the way back.}}

    {{t17|50 minutes · All levels · Up to 8 people}}

  2. {{t18|Tower}}

    {{t19|A frame of springs and bars fixed to the wall. Slower, heavier work for hips and shoulders, and the class we send stiff backs to first.}}

    {{t20|50 minutes · Level 2 · Up to 4 people}}

  3. {{t21|Mat}}

    {{t22|No machine, just your own weight and a mat. The original repertoire, taught slowly, and the one you can take home with you.}}

    {{t23|45 minutes · All levels · Up to 12 people}}

  4. {{t24|Ring}}

    {{t25|A flexible ring held between the hands or the knees. Short, focused classes for inner thighs, chest and arms, good after a long day at a desk.}}

    {{t26|30 minutes · All levels · Up to 10 people}}

{{/sec_classes}} {{#sec_method}}

{{t27|Six rules we teach by}}

{{t28|Breath}} {{t29|leads every movement, never the other way round.}} {{t30|Centre}} {{t31|is where it starts: the band from ribs to hips that holds you up.}} {{t32|Control}} {{t33|beats speed, so nothing swings and nothing is thrown.}} {{t34|Precision}} {{t35|beats repetition: five careful reps over twenty loose ones.}} {{t36|Concentration}} {{t37|keeps your mind on the muscle doing the work.}} {{t38|Flow}} {{t39|joins it all, so the class moves like one long exercise.}}

{{/sec_method}} {{#sec_fifty}}

{{t40|Fifty minutes, in order}}

{{t41|Every class follows the same arc, so your body learns its shape and your attention can stay on the work instead of on what comes next.}}

  1. {{t42|0}}
  2. {{t43|10}}
  3. {{t44|20}}
  4. {{t45|30}}
  5. {{t46|40}}
  6. {{t47|50 min}}

  1. {{t48|Minutes 0 to 5}}

    {{t49|The ⟦name⟧}}

    {{t50|Arm beats on the breath, in for five and out for five, to warm the trunk and find the centre before anything moves.}}

  2. {{t51|Minutes 5 to 12}}

    {{t52|Feet and legs}}

    {{t53|Feet on the bar, then in the straps: legs warmed and lined up before they carry any load.}}

  3. {{t54|Minutes 12 to 22}}

    {{t55|Arms in straps}}

    {{t56|Kneeling and seated work that opens the chest and gives the shoulders a job they rarely get.}}

  4. {{t57|Minutes 22 to 32}}

    {{t30|Centre}}

    {{t58|Planks, curls and the long stretch: the heaviest ten minutes, taken slowly and counted out loud.}}

  5. {{t59|Minutes 32 to 42}}

    {{t60|Length}}

    {{t61|Side work and long reaches that lengthen everything the centre block just shortened.}}

  6. {{t62|Minutes 42 to 50}}

    {{t63|Release}}

    {{t64|The spine rolled down one bone at a time, breath back to normal, nothing rushed on the way out of the door.}}
