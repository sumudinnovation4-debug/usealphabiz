{{t147|Questions}}

{{t148|Asked at the kitchen table}}

{{t149|The questions we hear on almost every first visit. Anything else, ask Dan on the phone.}}

{{t9|⟦phone⟧}}
  • {{t150|Do I need planning permission for a rear extension?}}

    {{t151|Many single-storey rear extensions fall under permitted development, but terraces and conservation areas have their own limits. We check on the first visit and handle the application or a lawful development certificate when it is needed.}}

  • {{t152|How long does a typical job take?}}

    {{t153|Bathrooms take four to six weeks, kitchens six to nine, lofts ten to twelve and rear extensions twelve to sixteen. The programme is drawn week by week before we start, and the diary tells you where we are against it.}}

  • {{t154|Can we stay in the house during the work?}}

    {{t155|Usually, yes. We seal off the work with dust screens, keep a temporary kitchen running when we can and plan the noisy and the no-water days with you in advance.}}

  • {{t156|How do payments work?}}

    {{t157|A small deposit books your start date. After that you pay in stages tied to finished work, never ahead of it, and five per cent is held until the snag list is empty.}}

  • {{t158|Are you insured and certified?}}

    {{t159|Yes. We carry public liability and contract works insurance, our electrics and gas work are certified, and every certificate goes into the handover folder with the drawings.}}
