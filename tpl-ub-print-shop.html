{{t153|Send it right, get it right.}}

{{t154|Four things we check on every file. Get them right and your job skips the fix-up queue.}}

  1. {{t155|3 mm bleed on every edge}}

    {{t156|Let colour run past the trim, so no white hairline shows after the cut.}}

  2. {{t157|Crop marks, or none at all}}

    {{t158|Put them outside the bleed, or leave them off and we add our own.}}

  3. {{t159|Words 5 mm inside the trim}}

    {{t160|Keep text and logos clear of the blade.}}

  4. {{t161|Colour at 300 dpi}}

    {{t162|CMYK is best; RGB is fine too, we convert it and show you a proof first.}}

{{t168|The questions we hear most, answered the way we would answer them across the desk.}}

{{t169|Do I need an appointment?}}

{{t170|No. Walk in any time we are open. For runs over a thousand sheets, ring ahead so the machine is free.}}

{{t171|Which files do you take?}}

{{t172|A print-ready PDF is best. We also open Word, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG and packaged InDesign files, and we will tell you if anything looks off.}}

{{t173|How fast is same day?}}

{{t174|Files in by 2 pm are printed, finished and on the shelf by close. Copies and prints from a stick are done while you wait.}}

{{t175|Can I see a proof first?}}

{{t176|Yes. Every job gets a printed proof on the real stock; it is free on runs of twenty or more and a pound otherwise.}}

{{t177|Do you deliver?}}

{{t178|Inside the ring road a bike courier brings it over the same evening. Anywhere else, we post it tracked the next morning.}}

{{t179|Can you fix my layout?}}

{{t180|Small fixes at the counter are free. A new layout, a poster or a menu from scratch is charged by the half hour.}}
