{{t43|Settling in, one step at a time}}

{{t44|Two weeks, four small steps. You stay as long as your child needs you, and step back only when Jess and your child are ready.}}

  1. {{t45|Days 1 and 2}}

    {{t46|Stay and play together}}

    {{t47|You come in with your child for an hour. Jess plays alongside you both and learns the songs, the snacks and the words for everything.}}

  2. {{t48|Days 3 and 4}}

    {{t49|A short goodbye}}

    {{t50|You say goodbye at the door and wait in the kitchen with a cup of tea. Twenty minutes, then back for a cuddle.}}

  3. {{t51|Days 5 to 7}}

    {{t52|A morning on their own}}

    {{t53|A whole morning with Jess and the room, lunch included. We send photos so you can see how it's going.}}

  4. {{t54|Week two}}

    {{t55|A full day, then home-time hugs}}

    {{t56|The first full day, with Jess close by. Most children have a favourite friend by the end of the week.}}

{{t79|Questions parents ask}}

{{t80|And if yours isn't here, ask us at an open morning or call the house: someone always picks up.}}

{{t81|What ages do you take?}}

{{t82|From twelve months until children start school, usually the September after they turn four.}}

{{t83|Do you take funded hours?}}

{{t84|Yes, 15 and 30 funded hours for every age we care for, term-time or stretched across the year. We take them off your bill before we send it.}}

{{t85|What should my child bring?}}

{{t86|Wellies, a spare set of clothes and a comforter if they have one. We provide nappies, wipes, sun cream and every meal and snack.}}

{{t87|How do you handle allergies?}}

{{t88|Our kitchen is nut-free. Rosa sits down with you before the first day and plans every allergy and diet, and each room has the list on the wall.}}

{{t89|Is there a waiting list?}}

{{t90|For Buds there often is. Register at an open morning and we will tell you honestly how long it is likely to be.}}

{{t91|When are you open?}}

{{t92|Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 18:00, 51 weeks a year. We close between Christmas and New Year.}}
