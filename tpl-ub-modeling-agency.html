{{t113|Before you walk in}}

{{t114|Questions we hear from applicants and their parents. Anything else, call the studio.}}

{{t115|Do I need professional photos?}}

{{t116|No. Phone photos in daylight are better: no filters, no make-up, hair off your face. We take the professional ones.}}

{{t117|Is there an age or height limit?}}

{{t118|We meet people from 14 upwards. Height matters for runway, not for commercial and beauty work, so apply whatever your height.}}

{{t119|I'm under 18. What do my parents need to do?}}

{{t120|A parent or guardian comes to the open call and signs any agreement with you. They are copied on every booking.}}

{{t121|What happens at the open call?}}

{{t122|A short chat, a few digital photos against a plain wall and a walk across the studio. It takes about twenty minutes.}}

{{t123|How soon will I hear back?}}

{{t124|Within ten working days, whether it is a yes or a no. We reply to every application.}}

{{t125|Can I be with another agency too?}}

{{t126|If you already have a mother agency, tell us. We can still place you abroad through them.}}
