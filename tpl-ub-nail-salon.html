{{t133|How long does a set last?}}

{{t134|Three weeks on most nails, up to four for a bare builder set. Refill before the growth gap reaches a millimetre and a half.}}

{{t135|Will gel damage my nails?}}

{{t136|Careless removal does, not the gel. We soak and lift, never drill into your own nail, and Ines runs bare sets for nails that need a rest.}}

{{t137|Can I bring my own design?}}

{{t138|Yes. Add a photo to your booking note so we can set aside time for the painting and pick the right artist.}}
