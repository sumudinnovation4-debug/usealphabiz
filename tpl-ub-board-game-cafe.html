{{t10|Questions}}

{{t175|Before you come}}

{{t176|Anything else, ring the café on}} {{t177|⟦phone⟧}}.

{{t178|Do we need to book?}}

{{t179|Walk-ins are welcome on weekdays. Book for Friday and Saturday evenings, and for groups of six or more.}}

{{t180|How long can we stay?}}

{{t181|As long as you like: the table fee covers the whole day. On busy evenings we ask big groups to keep to four hours.}}

{{t182|Can you teach us a game we've never played?}}

{{t183|That is what the guides are for, and it is included in the table fee. Ask as often as you like.}}

{{t184|Is it all right to bring children?}}

{{t185|Yes. Under-12s pay ₦1,500 Saturday mornings are set aside for families and the first-timers shelf is full of games for seven-year-olds.}}

{{t186|Can we bring our own game?}}

{{t187|Of course. The table fee is the same, and a guide will help you set it up.}}

{{t188|Do you cater for allergies?}}

{{t189|Tell us when you order. The kitchen keeps gluten-free bread and vegan cheese, and the full allergen list is at the counter.}}

{{t190|Is the café step-free?}}

{{t191|The ground floor, the toilets and the long table room are step-free. The quiet room upstairs is reached by stairs.}}
