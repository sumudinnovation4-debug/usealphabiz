{{t144|We Keep}}
{{t145|It Dark}}

{{t146|A dark sky is the one thing we could never build back. These rules hold on every ridge, and they are written into our leases with the land.}}

  1. {{t147|No light above knee height, anywhere on the ridge.}}

    {{t148|Path lamps point at the ground and stop at your ankles.}}

  2. {{t149|Amber bulbs only, and few of them.}}

    {{t150|Warm, dim glass that leaves your night vision alone.}}

  3. {{t151|Shared lamps go off at ten, every night.}}

    {{t152|Your own lamps stay yours, on a timer you can override.}}

  4. {{t153|No generators, no drones, no speakers.}}

    {{t154|The loudest thing on the ridge should be the wind.}}

  5. {{t155|Nothing built that shows from the next ridge.}}

    {{t156|Every stay turns its glass away from the others.}}

{{t186|Before You}}
{{t187|Drive Out}}

{{t188|Still unsure about something? Mara answers the phone from eight to eight, every day.}}

{{t14|+1 760 000 0000}}
{{t189|Is there wifi or phone signal?}}

{{t190|Not in the stays. Each ridge has a small cabin at the gate with wifi and a landline for emergencies, and your host is always reachable.}}

{{t191|What happens if it is cloudy?}}

{{t192|Clouds come and go fast up here. If a whole stay is overcast, tell us before checkout and we give you a free night to use within a year.}}

{{t193|Are the stays warm in winter?}}

{{t194|Yes. Cabins and suites have wood stoves, domes and pods have quiet electric heating and heavy duvets, and every bed has a hot water bottle.}}

{{t195|Can we bring children or a dog?}}

{{t196|Children are welcome in the canvas suites at Juniper Mesa. Dogs can stay in the Rim Cabins at Snowline for $30 a night.}}

{{t197|Is there running water and a real toilet?}}

{{t198|Every stay has its own bathroom with a hot shower and a flushing toilet. Water comes from the ridge's own well and is safe to drink.}}

{{t199|What is the cancellation policy?}}

{{t200|Cancel up to fourteen days before arrival for a full refund. After that we move your dates once, free, within the next twelve months.}}
