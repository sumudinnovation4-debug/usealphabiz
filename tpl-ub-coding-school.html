{{t71|Built around the job you still have.}}

{{t72|Three fixed sessions a week, the same for all twenty-four weeks, so the rest of your life can plan around them. Then about six hours on your own, whenever the house is quiet.}}

  1. {{t73|Tuesday}}

    {{t74|18:30–21:00 · Live class}}

    {{t75|A mentor teaches the week's idea to twelve of you, in the room or on a call. Recorded, in case the bus is late.}}

  2. {{t76|Thursday}}

    {{t77|18:30–21:00 · Pairing night}}

    {{t78|You and one classmate, one problem, one keyboard, swapping every twenty minutes. The quickest way we know to stop being stuck.}}

  3. {{t79|Saturday}}

    {{t80|10:00–15:00 · Studio day}}

    {{t81|Build the week's project with mentors walking the room. Lunch is on us, and children are welcome in the quiet room.}}

{{t164|Questions adults actually ask.}}

{{t165|Something else? Call}} {{t166|⟦phone⟧}} {{t167|on a weekday evening and a mentor will answer.}}

{{t168|I'm fifty-two. Is that too old?}}

{{t169|Our oldest graduate was sixty-three when she started, and the average age in the room is thirty-eight. Employers who hire our juniors ask about the work, not the birthday.}}

{{t170|Do I need to be good at maths?}}

{{t171|No. You need to be able to stay with a problem for twenty minutes before asking for help, and then to ask. That is most of the job.}}

{{t172|Can I keep my full-time job?}}

{{t173|Most people do. Plan on about fourteen hours a week: the three sessions and roughly six hours on your own.}}

{{t174|What computer do I need?}}

{{t175|Anything made in the last six years with 8 GB of memory. If you don't have one, we lend you a laptop for the whole course.}}

{{t176|What if I fall behind?}}

{{t177|Every module has a catch-up Saturday. If life properly happens, you can pause and rejoin the next cohort at the same module, once, at no cost.}}

{{t178|Do you help me find work afterwards?}}

{{t179|For a year after demo night: CV and portfolio reviews, mock interviews with mentors and introductions to the employers who hired our last cohorts.}}
