{{t109|A quiet house, by rule}}

{{t110|Stress is what makes cats ill on holiday. So the house is run for silence, distance and fresh air, and four rules are never bent.}}

  • {{t111|No dogs.}}

    {{t112|Not as guests, not as staff, not visiting. The house never smells of dog.}}

  • {{t113|One cat.}}

    {{t114|One cat per room. Siblings share only if they share at home.}}

  • {{t115|Own air.}}

    {{t116|Every room has its own vent, so nothing passes from one room to the next.}}

  • {{t117|Vet close.}}

{{t122|Which vaccinations are needed?}}

{{t123|Cat flu and feline panleukopenia, given at least two weeks before arrival. Bring the passport on the first day; we copy it and give it back.}}

{{t124|Can two cats share a room?}}

{{t125|Yes, if they live together at home. The Ledge and the Tower fit two; the second cat pays half.}}

{{t126|What about medication?}}

{{t127|Pills, drops and insulin are given at no extra cost. Write the schedule on the booking form and bring enough for the whole stay.}}

{{t128|Is there a minimum stay?}}

{{t129|Two nights. On weekdays we also take day guests from eight to six, for owners who are moving house or renovating.}}

{{t130|When can I drop off and pick up?}}

{{t131|Every day from 8:00 to 11:00 and from 17:00 to 19:00. On Sundays only in the evening.}}
