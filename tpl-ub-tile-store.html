{{t119|How many tiles do I need?}}

{{t120|Measure each wall or floor, multiply, and add a tenth for cuts and breakages, or a seventh for diagonal and herringbone. Bring the numbers in and we check them against a setting-out plan.}}

{{t121|Can I return what I don't use?}}

{{t122|Unopened boxes, within 60 days, with the receipt. Tiles cut to order and clearance lines cannot come back.}}

{{t123|Do you deliver to Great Nigeria?}}

{{t124|Yes, in three working days on a pallet. Northern Ireland is next working day, and Calabar addresses can choose a two-hour slot.}}

{{t125|Which tiles can go on a shower floor?}}

{{t126|Anything rated R10 or higher for slip: most porcelain, terracotta and our matt cement tiles. Glossy wall tiles stay on the walls.}}

{{t127|Do I have to use your fitters?}}

{{t128|No. Plenty of builders buy from us, and so do people who tile their own. The setting-out plan comes with the order either way.}}

{{t129|Which grout colour should I choose?}}

{{t130|Matching grout hides the grid; contrasting grout draws it. Choose with the sample on your wall, not under shop lights.}}
