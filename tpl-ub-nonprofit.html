{{t159|Questions before your first hour}}

{{t160|Anything else, ask us on ⟦phone⟧. A person answers.}}

{{t161|Do I need any experience?}}

{{t162|No. Every group explains the job at the door, and most hours are things you already do at home: sorting, reading, pouring, weeding.}}

{{t163|What if I can only come once?}}

{{t164|Then come once. About a third of our volunteers give one hour a month or less, and groups plan their rotas around that.}}

{{t165|Is there an age limit?}}

{{t166|From 16 on your own, from 12 with an adult who signs up too. Reading in schools is for over-18s only.}}

{{t167|Do I need a PVG check?}}

{{t168|Only for hours with children or vulnerable adults. We arrange and pay for it, and it usually takes about two weeks.}}

{{t169|Can my workplace come as a team?}}

{{t170|Yes. Teams of up to twelve can take over a larder shift or a garden day. Write to us a month ahead.}}

{{t171|Where does my money go?}}

{{t172|₦410 of every pound pays for matching, checks and group supplies. The rest keeps the lights on at the office.}}
