{{t167|Questions}}

{{t168|What people ask on the survey, answered before it.}}

{{t169|Do you need to chase walls or lift floors?}}

{{t170|In most homes, no. We use existing back boxes and ceiling voids, and wireless controls where a cable cannot reach. New builds and renovations get a fully wired system while the walls are open.}}

{{t171|What happens if the internet goes down?}}

{{t172|Everything keeps working. Scenes, schedules and plates run on a controller in the house; the internet only adds the app away from home and our remote checks.}}

{{t173|Can we keep our switches and radiators?}}

{{t174|Usually. Most radiators take a smart valve in minutes, and we can fit dimmers behind your current plates or match new plates to them.}}

{{t175|How long does an install take?}}

{{t176|Essentials takes about three days on site. A whole house is six to ten working days, phased so you can live there throughout.}}

{{t177|Are we tied to you afterwards?}}

{{t178|No. We choose open, well-supported systems and explain why in your design. At handover you get every password, drawing and device list.}}

{{t179|What does aftercare cost after ten years?}}

{{t180|Year eleven onwards is an optional plan at ₦7,000 a month, covering remote checks, the annual visit and call-outs.}}
