{{t75|From the bed to the wall}}

{{t76|A stone is chosen, squared, worked and set by the same few hands. Nothing is cut until it has been drawn full size.}}

  1. {{t77|Step 1}}

    {{t78|Survey and template}}

    {{t79|We measure the opening or the decayed stone, draw the profile full size and cut a zinc template from it.}}

  2. {{t80|Step 2}}

    {{t81|Sawn on its bed}}

    {{t82|The block is chosen for colour and grain and sawn square, marked so it will be laid the way it lay in the ground.}}

  3. {{t83|Step 3}}

    {{t84|Worked at the banker}}

    {{t85|Mouldings are worked by hand with drag, claw and chisel until the profile meets the template along its whole length.}}

  4. {{t86|Step 4}}

    {{t87|Signed and set}}

    {{t88|The mason cuts their mark on a hidden face, and the stone is fixed in lime by the team that made it.}}

{{t137|Lime, never cement}}

{{t138|An old wall is built to breathe. The mortar should be the weakest part of it, so that water and frost take the joint and leave the stone alone.}}

{{t18|Lime mortar}}
{{t139|Softer than the stone, so the joint wears instead of the face. Lets damp out. Raked and renewed in a day.}}
{{t140|Cement mortar}}
{{t141|Harder than the stone, so the stone wears instead. Holds damp behind the face until frost blows it off.}}

{{t142|We mix our own mortars from hot lime, sand and crushed stone to match the colour of each wall, and we rake out the cement others left behind.}}

{{t150|Asked on site}}

{{t151|The questions we hear most from the bottom of a ladder. Anything else, ask the yard.}}

{{t152|Do you work on listed buildings?}}

{{t153|Most of our work is listed. We prepare method statements and drawings for consent and work to the conservation officer's conditions.}}

{{t154|How far do you travel?}}

{{t155|Our fixing teams cover Wiltshire, Abeokuta, Somerset and Gloucestershire. We cut stone for jobs anywhere and send it with fixing drawings.}}

{{t156|Can you match my stone?}}

{{t157|Usually. We bring samples to site, and if the original quarry has closed we find the nearest bed in colour, grain and the way it weathers.}}

{{t158|How long does a memorial take?}}

{{t159|Allow eight to twelve weeks from an approved drawing. Churchyard rules can add time; we make the application for you.}}

{{t160|Will you repoint in cement?}}

{{t161|No. We rake out cement where it is doing harm and repoint in a lime mortar mixed to match the original.}}

{{t162|What does a site visit cost?}}

{{t163|The first visit and a written quote are free within thirty miles of Corsham. Further afield we charge travel only.}}
