{{t77|Four}} {{t78|promises}}

{{t79|Written by parents in 1987 and still pinned to the clubhouse door. Every coach signs them before their first session.}}

  1. {{t80|Everybody plays.}}

    {{t81|Up to under 12, every child gets equal minutes. After that, nobody plays less than half a match.}}

  2. {{t82|No trials under 13.}}

    {{t83|You don’t earn a place in a squad. You turn up, you train, and you’re in the team.}}

  3. {{t84|First kit is free.}}

    {{t85|Shirt, shorts and socks are on us for the first season. Boots come from the Thursday swap.}}

  4. {{t86|Coaches who stay.}}

    {{t87|Our coaches stay with their squad for at least two seasons, and most stay a lot longer.}}

{{t194|Parents}} {{t195|ask}}

{{t196|My child has never played. Is that a problem?}}

{{t197|Not at all. Most Mini Colts have never kicked a ball in a team, and every session starts with games that need no skill whatsoever.}}

{{t198|What should they bring to the first session?}}

{{t199|Shin pads, a water bottle and trainers or boots. We lend boots, bibs and shin pads to anyone who needs them, no questions asked.}}

{{t200|Do you hold trials?}}

{{t201|Not below under 13. From under 13 the youth section runs two levels, and players move between them through the season.}}

{{t202|How are the coaches checked?}}

{{t203|Every coach holds an FA badge, an in-date DBS check and the FA safeguarding certificate. Two adults are at every session.}}

{{t204|Can parents stay and watch?}}

{{t205|Please do. The touchline is behind the rope, the tea hatch opens at nine and the covered stand is there for wet days.}}

{{t206|How can I help the club?}}

{{t207|Run a line, wash a kit bag, staff the hatch or take a coaching badge on us. Every role is voluntary and every hour counts.}}
