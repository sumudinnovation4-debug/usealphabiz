{{t134|Before you come}}

{{t135|Anything else, ask the front desk or write to us.}}

{{t136|Is entry really free?}}

{{t137|Yes. The six halls are free for everyone, every day we are open. Booking a free ticket only helps us plan busy days; you can always walk in.}}

{{t138|Can I bring a pushchair or a wheelchair?}}

{{t139|Every hall is step-free, with lifts to each floor. Pushchairs are welcome everywhere, and we lend wheelchairs and folding stools at the front desk.}}

{{t140|Can I take photographs?}}

{{t141|Yes, without flash, in the halls and The Colour of Flight. Tripods are fine on quiet mornings.}}

{{t142|Will you identify something I found?}}

{{t143|Bring it to the identification desk on the first Saturday of each month, 11:00 to 15:00. Bones, shells, fossils, insects and pressed plants all welcome.}}

{{t144|Is there somewhere to eat?}}

{{t145|The café on the landing serves lunch until 15:00, and the picnic room on the lower ground floor is open to school groups and families.}}

{{t146|How do I give a specimen to the museum?}}

{{t147|Write to the collections team with a photograph and where it was found. We accept most donations that come with a record of their place and date.}}
