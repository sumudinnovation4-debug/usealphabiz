    {{t92|The front row gets talked to}}

{{t174|Asked}} {{t175|before}}

{{t176|Answered with a straight face, for once.}}

{{t177|Is there an age limit?}}

{{t178|Every show is 18+ and we ask for ID at the door. Some of the language would make your nan blush. Some of it would make her laugh.}}

{{t179|Are the seats allocated?}}

{{t180|No. Doors open half an hour before the show and it’s first in, first seated. The front row is a choice, not a punishment. Mostly.}}

{{t181|Will I get picked on?}}

{{t182|Only if you sit in the front row, arrive late or wear a hat indoors. Sit in the middle and you’re safe, unless you laugh like a goose.}}

{{t183|Is the arch accessible?}}

{{t184|Yes: a step-free entrance from the wharf, four wheelchair spaces beside the stage, an accessible toilet and a hearing loop. Tell us when you book.}}

{{t185|Can I swap or refund tickets?}}

{{t186|We swap tickets to another night up to 24 hours before the show. If we cancel, you get your money back the same day.}}

{{t187|Can I eat there?}}

{{t188|Fries, toasties and pickles from the bar until 21:00 and again after the show. Nothing that crunches during a set, please.}}
