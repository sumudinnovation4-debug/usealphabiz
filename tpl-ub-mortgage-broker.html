{{t157|Do you really not charge a fee?}}

{{t158|For most residential mortgages, no. The lender pays us a fee when your mortgage completes, usually 0.35% to 0.45% of the loan, and we tell you the exact amount in writing before you apply. Complex cases, such as limited-company buy-to-let, carry a ₦247,500 fee that we agree with you first.}}

{{t159|Will talking to you affect my credit score?}}

{{t160|No. We use a soft search to see your file the way a lender will, and it leaves no footprint that other lenders can see. A hard search happens only when you decide to apply.}}

{{t161|How much deposit do I need?}}

{{t162|Some lenders accept 5%, but 10% or 15% usually unlocks noticeably better rates. We show you the difference in pounds a month for your own numbers, so you can decide whether waiting to save more is worth it.}}

{{t163|I'm self-employed. Can I still get a mortgage?}}

{{t164|Yes. Most lenders want two years of accounts or tax calculations, and some accept one. Daniel works with contractors and company directors every week and knows which lenders read accounts sensibly.}}

{{t165|How long from first call to mortgage offer?}}

{{t166|An agreement in principle usually takes a day. Once your offer on a home is accepted, a full mortgage offer typically takes two to four weeks, depending on the lender and the valuation.}}

{{t167|What happens when my fixed rate ends?}}

{{t168|We call you six months before, compare the market again and reserve a new rate. If rates fall before your switch date, we re-book the lower one, so you never spend a month on the standard variable rate.}}
