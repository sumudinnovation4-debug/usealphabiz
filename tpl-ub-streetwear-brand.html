{{t87|When do drops land?}}

{{t88|Every other Friday at 6 pm CET, online and in the studio shop at the same minute. The calendar above lists the next five.}}

{{t89|How do I send a doodle?}}

{{t90|Photograph it in daylight and email it to doodles@scrawl.example before the last Sunday of the month. Paper, napkins and phone sketches all count.}}

{{t91|What if my doodle is picked?}}

{{t92|You get a fee, ten percent of the print's sales, a box of the finished pieces and your name on every tag.}}

{{t93|Shipping and returns}}

{{t94|We ship worldwide in recycled mailers. Returns are free within 30 days, unworn and unwashed.}}

{{t95|How do the sizes run?}}

{{t96|Boxy and a touch long. Take your usual size for the intended fit, one down for a closer one.}}
