{{t76|What you carry}}

{{t77|The ridge stays above 2,400 metres for sixteen kilometres. In June it can be 24 °C in Orvalle and 2 °C on Kessel Tor. Everything here is checked at the start and can be checked again at any aid station.}}

  • {{t78|Waterproof jacket}}{{t79|With a hood and taped seams}}
  • {{t80|A warm layer}}{{t81|Long sleeves, fleece or wool}}
  • {{t82|Legs covered}}{{t83|Tights or trousers down to the knee or below}}
  • {{t84|Cap and gloves}}{{t85|The ridge can be cold at noon}}
  • {{t86|One litre of water}}{{t87|Bottles or a bladder, full at every aid station}}
  • {{t88|Your own cup}}{{t89|The aid stations have no cups}}
  • {{t90|Food for two hours}}{{t91|Aid stations are up to 9 km apart}}
  • {{t92|Emergency blanket}}{{t93|At least 1.4 × 2 metres}}
  • {{t94|Whistle}}{{t95|Most vests have one on the chest strap}}
  • {{t96|Phone}}{{t97|Charged, with the race office number saved}}

{{t207|Before you apply}}

{{t208|The questions the race office hears most, answered once.}}

{{t209|Something else?}} {{t210|Ask the office}}

{{t211|Do I need a qualifying race?}}

{{t212|No. We ask Long runners to have finished one mountain race of 40 km or more; you tick a box and we trust you.}}

{{t213|Are poles allowed?}}

{{t214|Yes, in all three races. Pack them before the scree, where you will want your hands.}}

{{t215|What if the weather turns?}}

{{t216|The ridge has a lower route along the 2,000 m contour. If the 03:00 forecast shows storms the Long runs it; if a storm arrives during the day, marshals turn runners at the col.}}

{{t217|What are the cut-offs?}}

{{t218|Long: the col at 12:30, Kessel Tor at 15:40, the finish at 21:40. Half: the col at 14:00, the finish at 19:30. Vertical: Kessel Hut at 20:10.}}

{{t219|Can I give my place away?}}

{{t220|Once, until 1 May, through the race office. The trail day goes with the place.}}

{{t221|How do I get to Orvalle?}}

{{t222|Train to the valley town, then the post bus, 40 minutes. On race day extra buses run from 03:30; the village has no parking on Saturday.}}

{{t223|Pacers or dogs?}}

{{t224|Neither on the course. Supporters can meet you at Pra Bas, at Sella and in the square.}}

{{t225|Where do I sleep?}}

{{t226|Orvalle has 300 beds. The school gym opens on Friday night: mattress, shower and breakfast at 03:30 for €18.}}
