{{t162|Asked at the counter}}

{{t163|Do you really sell screws one at a time?}}

{{t164|Yes. Every drawer in aisle 2 is priced by the one, by the ten and by the box, so you buy what the job needs and not a tin of spares for the shed.}}

{{t165|Can you match a paint colour from a chip?}}

{{t166|Bring a flake the size of a fingernail. The scanner reads it and we mix it in about ten minutes, in any finish from flat matt to full gloss.}}

{{t167|Do you deliver?}}

{{t168|Within three miles on Tuesdays and Fridays. It costs ₦2,500 and is free over ₦30,000 timber and sheet materials included.}}

{{t169|How does tool hire work?}}

{{t170|Reserve by phone, collect from three on Friday and return by ten on Monday. We take a refundable deposit and photo ID, and show you how the tool works before it leaves.}}

{{t171|Can I bring things back?}}

{{t172|Unused and in its packet, within 30 days, with the receipt. Cut keys, mixed paint and cut timber are made for you, so they stay with you.}}

{{t173|Which keys can you cut?}}

{{t174|Most house, shed and padlock keys while you wait. Security and car keys are ordered in and take three to five days.}}
