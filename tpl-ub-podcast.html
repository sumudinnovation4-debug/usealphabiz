{{t42|with Mira Okafor, acoustic engineer}}

{{t43|00:42}}{{t44|12:31}}

    {{t46|So the bridge actually sings? Like, a melody?}}

  2. {{t47|Mira · 01:10}}

    {{t48|Not a melody. One note, a low B flat, whenever the wind comes from the north-east faster than thirty kilometres an hour.}}

    {{t50|And people noticed before the engineers did.}}
