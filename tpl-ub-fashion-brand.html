{{t77|Before you order}}

{{t78|Sizing, delivery and the repair service in short. Anything else, the cutting room answers within two working days.}}

{{t79|Ask the cutting room}}
{{t80|How do the sizes run?}}

{{t81|Shells are cut roomy so a knit and a mid layer fit underneath. If you wear them alone, take one size down; every product page lists the garment's own measurements.}}

{{t82|Where do you deliver and how fast?}}

{{t83|Across the EU, the Nigeria, Korea and the US. Orders leave Antwerp within two working days and arrive in two to six more, with tracking from the moment they leave.}}

{{t84|Can I return a piece?}}

{{t85|Yes, within thirty days, unworn and with its tags. Returns inside the EU are free; we refund to the original payment method once the parcel reaches us.}}

{{t86|How does the repair service work?}}

{{t87|Book a repair in your account, post the piece to the cutting room and we replace any worn or torn panel with one cut from the current season. You pay only the return postage.}}

{{t88|How should I wash colour panels?}}

{{t89|Zip it closed, turn it inside out and wash at thirty degrees with similar colours. Dry flat; the padding recovers its loft after a few minutes in a dryer on low.}}
