  • {{t100|Tea, not powder}}

{{t144|Is anything vegan?}}

{{t145|Yes. Every tea, oat milk, all fruit bases and our brown-sugar pearls are vegan. Only fresh milk, cheese foam and taro paste are not.}}

{{t146|How much caffeine is in a cup?}}

{{t147|A regular jasmine or Assam cup has about as much as a small coffee. Butterfly pea and every fruit base have none.}}

{{t148|Where can I see allergens?}}

{{t149|The full allergen sheet sits on the counter and on the order screen. Popping pearls contain seaweed extract; taro paste contains milk.}}
