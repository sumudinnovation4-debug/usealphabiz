{{t46|From the first phone call to the weekly routine, here's what happens and how much of your time each step takes.}}

  1. {{t47|Step 1 · 15 minutes}}

    {{t48|A free call}}

    {{t49|Tell us what you've noticed. We'll say honestly whether to wait and watch or book an assessment, and nobody will chase you either way.}}

  2. {{t50|Step 2 · 75 minutes}}

    {{t51|A play-based assessment}}

    {{t52|On the floor with toys, books and bubbles while we listen and watch. You stay in the room the whole time.}}

  3. {{t53|Step 3 · within a week}}

    {{t54|A plan you can read}}

    {{t55|Two or three goals in plain English, how we'll work on them, and what "better" will look like at home.}}

  4. {{t56|Step 4 · every week}}

    {{t57|Sessions, then ten minutes a day}}

    {{t58|Forty-five minutes with the same therapist, plus a short game for each day. We review the plan every six sessions.}}

{{/sec_visit}} {{#sec_play}}

{{t59|Play is the method, not the reward.}}

{{t60|Children talk most when they're busy having fun. Every game in our playrooms is chosen for the sounds and words it makes easy.}}

  • {{t61|Sock puppets.}} {{t62|Taking turns — my go, your go — without anyone being told to.}}

  • {{t63|A toy phone.}} {{t64|First conversations, with no pressure to look at anyone.}}

  • {{t65|Bubbles.}} {{t66|Round lips for "oo" and "w", and a very good reason to say "more".}}

  • {{t67|Den reading.}} {{t68|Naming, pointing and waiting for the next page.}}

{{/sec_play}} {{#sec_home}}

{{t137|Fees, plainly}}

{{t138|No packages to sign up to, and no charge for cancelling with 48 hours' notice. You pay after each session.}}

{{t139|Do we need a referral?}}

{{t140|No. Parents can book with us directly. If your GP or health visitor has written to you, bring the letter along.}}

{{t141|What if my child won't join in?}}

{{t142|That's common, and it tells us something too. The first session is play on their terms, and you stay in the room.}}

{{t143|How many sessions will we need?}}

{{t144|It varies. We review the plan every six sessions and tell you honestly when it's time to stop.}}

{{t145|Can we claim the cost back?}}

{{t146|Some health insurers cover therapy from an HCPC-registered therapist. You'll get an itemised receipt for every session.}}
