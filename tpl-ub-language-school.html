  1. {{t39|A1}}

    {{t40|First words}}

    {{t41|Greet people, order, ask the way and spell your name.}}

  2. {{t42|A2}}

    {{t43|Getting by}}

    {{t44|Talk about your week, book a room and sort out a mix-up.}}

  3. {{t45|B1}}

    {{t46|Holding a conversation}}

    {{t47|Tell a story, give an opinion and follow a podcast.}}

  4. {{t48|B2}}

{{t106|I have never learned a language. Where do I start?}}

{{t107|At a free class. You sit in on a beginners’ group, the teacher hears you say a few things, and we suggest a level and an evening that suits you.}}

{{t108|What if I miss a class?}}

{{t109|Every class streams, so you can join from home, or come to another group at the same level that week.}}

{{t110|How big are the groups?}}

{{t111|Never more than six people. Small enough that everyone talks in every class, big enough to have someone to argue with.}}

{{t112|Who are the teachers?}}

{{t113|People who grew up speaking the language and trained with us to teach it. Most have lived in Abuja for years and teach the language as it is spoken now.}}

{{t114|Do you teach teams at work?}}

{{t115|Yes. We run courses for teams in your office or ours, built around the conversations your people actually have.}}
